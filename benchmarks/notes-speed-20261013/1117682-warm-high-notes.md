# Stripes on a טלית

## הקדמה

- **Q:** where do stripes on a טלית come from, is it a מנהג ישראל or just something we do
    - Many people have black stripes, some have blue, some have white, and some have Joseph's technicolor dreamcoat with all different colors

## Three sources for stripes

- **פרי מגדים סימן ט׳:**
    - Suggests it is a זכר לתכלת because for many years the Jewish people didn't have תכלת
        - **Q:** now that I have תכלת should I not have stripes on my טלית
            - **A:** you are still allowed to have stripes
        - Only a wool טלית קטן will have black stripes, but a cotton טלית קטן doesn't, because תכלת only works on wool
            - תכלת is by definition wool, so if it will be a זכר to תכלת it has to be on a wool בגד
            - Not that they couldn't make black cotton, since black cotton t-shirts exist
- **זוהר:**
    - על פי קבלה black represents the מדה of גבורה and white represents the מדה of חסד or רחמים
        - So this fusion of גבורה and רחמים should be represented in the טלית
            - Therefore not just white, which is the primary color, but also black or blue as the secondary color
- **משנה מסכת ברכות:**
    - Talks about the different זמנים in the morning, and the earliest זמן for קריאת שמע is when a person can be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
        - If you have a white בגד with a תכלת stripe right in front of you, you can see whether you are able to be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
        - So maybe it was included not as the מצוה דאורייתא of תכלת but to function for one thing תכלת helped with, telling the זמן for קריאת שמע

## Black stripes

- Most common nowadays is black
- **רמב״ם הלכות ציצית פרק ב׳:**
    - Held that the דם החלזון they used was a black דם, although he seems to understand that once it went through the process it actually produced a blue dye and not a black dye
- **גמ׳ מנחות:**
    - Says תכלת דומה לים and ים דומה לרקיע, so תכלת is supposed to be the color of the ים or רקיע, but doesn't say explicitly at what time of day
        - The רקיע has a very different color during broad daylight than at nighttime
            - **רש״י:** it is the רקיע toward evening time, which would mean a much darker blue
            - **רמב״ם:** it is the middle of the day
        - So if you hold תכלת is supposed to be black, then the stripes should be black, or even a blackish very dark blue that almost appears black
- **תורה as אש שחורה על גבי אש לבנה:**
    - White is purity and innocence, and the black lines or letters of the תורה are the אש שחורה on top of the אש לבנה, and the טלית is representative of that
        - Fits because the ציצית themselves are supposed to remind us of all תרי״ג מצות, so the בגד upon which you put the ציצית should somehow represent the entirety of תורה
            - **רש״י בחומש:** ציצית represent all תרי״ג מצות
            - **גמ׳:** ציצית remind us of all תרי״ג מצות

## Blue stripes

- Blue used to be the more common מנהג, but it grew out of favor in more contemporary times
    - **Ex:** David Wolffsohn, a prominent Zionist businessman, had to figure out what flag to hang in the Congress Hall after the design proposed by Theodor Herzl wasn't gaining support
        - He wrote, at the behest of our leader Herzl I came to Basel to make preparations for the Zionist Congress, and one problem contained something of the essence of the Jewish problem, what flag would we hang, then an idea struck me, we have a flag and it is blue and white, the טלית with which we wrap ourselves when we pray, that is our symbol, so all I have to do is take the טלית out of the bag, slap a מגן דוד on it, and that is our flag
        - So the assumption was that the טלית had blue stripes on it

## White stripes

- On יום כיפור people wear white because it is a symbol of purity, אם יהיו חטאיכם כשנים כשלג ילבינו, that even though we may have sins we hope they become whitened and purified
- Even not on יום כיפור there is a fairly common ספרדי practice to have white stripes
- **ברכי יוסף בשם מהרש״ל:**
    - Writes in שיורי ברכה that יש אומרים the טלית should be white
        - Could be partially based on what the שולחן ערוך writes
            - **שולחן ערוך סימן ט׳ סעיף ה׳:** יש אומרים שצריך לעשות הציצית מצבע הטלית, והמדקדקים נוהגים כן
            - **רמ״א:** והאשכנזים לא נהגו לעשות הציצית רק לבנים, אף בבגדים צבועים, ואין לשנות
        - Put two things together, that the ציצית are supposed to match the בגד, and our מנהג is we only use white ציצית, so then the בגד should be white
- **בן איש חי פרשת נח:**
    - על פי קבלה many ספרדים have דווקא fully white, יזהר לעשות הטליות והחוטים של הציציות הכל לבן, בין בטלית גדול בין בטלית קטן, רמז לדבר עטוף בלבן
        - But טליתות שיש בהן צבע מצדדים לנוי, לא אכפת, if there is a little color on the side to make it more beautiful that doesn't bother him
            - כי הצבע הוא מעט מן המעט בגוף הטלית דרובו לבן, it is mostly white, so that fulfills the דין
- Even so it is not absolute, since you find pictures of famous ספרדי rabbis with black stripes
    - **Ex:** רב בנציון אבא שאול with a טלית with black stripes
    - **Ex:** רב כדורי with a טלית with black stripes
    - **Ex:** רב עובדיה mostly white, but from the earlier years some pictures with black stripes also
