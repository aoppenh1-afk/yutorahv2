# Stripes on a טלית

## הקדמה

- Many have black stripes on טלית, some blue, some white, some all colors like Joseph's technicolor dreamcoat
- **Q:** where does this come from, is it a מנהג ישראל or just something we do
- Three possibilities mentioned by ספרים for very concept of stripes
- **פרי מגדים סימן ט׳ הלכות ציצית**
    - suggests stripes are זכר לתכלת because for many years Jewish people didn't have תכלת
        - **Q:** now that I have תכלת should I not have stripes on טלית
            - **A:** still allowed to have stripes
        - Only wool טלית קטן has black stripes but cotton טלית קטן doesn't
            - **Q:** why can't they make black stripe on cotton, they figured out black cotton t-shirts
            - **A:** because תכלת only works on wool, by definition wool, so black stripes as זכר to תכלת must be on wool בגד
- **זוהר על פי קבלה**
    - black represents מדה of גבורה and white represents מדה of חסד or רחמים
        - fusion of גבורה and רחמים should be represented in טלית
            - so not just white as primary color but also black or blue as secondary color
- **משנה מסכת ברכות**
    - discusses morning זמנים and earliest זמן to say קריאת שמע when a person can be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
        - **Q:** how know if able to be מבחין
        - **A:** if have right in front you white בגד and תכלת stripe, can see if able to be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
    - maybe included not only as זכר to תכלת but to function for one of things תכלת helped with, telling זמן for קריאת שמע
        - can't function as מצוה דאורייתא of תכלת

## Why black stripes?

- Most common nowadays is black
- **רמב״ם הלכות ציצית פרק ב׳**
    - held דם החלזון used was black דם
        - although seems once through process it actually produced blue dye and not black dye
- **גמ׳ מנחות**
    - תכלת דומה לים and ים דומה לרקיע, so in some way supposed to be color of ים or רקיע
        - doesn't say explicitly at what time of day
            - רקיע has very different color in broad daylight than at nighttime
                - **רש״י:** seems to understand רקיע toward evening time, which would be much darker blue
                - **רמב״ם:** learns middle of day
    - so idea of black is if hold תכלת supposed to be black then stripes supposed to be black
        - or even if תכלת were blackish very dark blue that almost appears black
- **תורה as אש שחורה על גבי אש לבנה**
    - sense of white לבנה as purity and innocence and black lines or letters of תורה as אש שחורה on top of אש לבנה, representative of that
        - ציצית themselves supposed to represent all תרי״ג מצות
            - **רש״י in חומש:** says supposed to represent all תרי״ג מצות
            - **גמ׳:** says supposed to remind us of all תרי״ג מצות
        - so maybe בגד upon which put ציצית should somehow represent entirety of תורה

## Why blue stripes?

- Blue used to be more common מנהג but grew out of favor in contemporary times
- **Ex:** David Wolffsohn, prominent Zionist businessman, when design proposed by Theodor Herzl for Israeli flag wasn't gaining support
    - wrote at behest of Herzl came to Basel to make preparations for Zionist Congress, among many problems was what flag to hang in Congress Hall
        - idea struck that we have flag and it's blue and white, the טלית with which we wrap ourselves when we daven, that is our symbol
            - so take טלית out of bag, slap מגן דוד on it, and that's our flag
    - concept became Israeli flag, with assumption טלית had blue stripes on it

## Why white stripes?

- Certainly on יום כיפור people wear white as symbol of purity
    - אם יהיו חטאיכם כשנים כשלג ילבינו, even though may have sins we hope becomes whitened and purified
- Even not on יום כיפור, fairly common ספרדי practice to have white stripes
- **ברכי יוסף in שיורי ברכה in name of מהרש״ל**
    - **יש אומרים:** טלית should be white
- **שולחן ערוך סימן ט׳ סעיף ה׳**
    - יש אומרים שצריך לעשות הציצית מצבע הטלית, והמדקדקים נוהגים כן
        - **רמ״א:** והאשכנזים לא נהגו לעשות הציצית רק לבנים, אף בבגדים צבועים, ואין לשנות
    - put two things together, ציצית supposed to match בגד and our מנהג is only white ציצית, so בגד should be white
        - so practice is to have white
- Also על פי קבלה many ספרדים have דווקא fully white
- **בן איש חי פרשת נח**
    - doesn't bother him that there are stripes of different color
        - יזהר לעשות הטליות והחוטים של הציציות הכל לבן, בין בטלית גדול בין בטלית קטן, רמז לדבר עטוף בלבן
        - וטליתות שיש בהן צבע מצדדים לנוי, לא אכפת, if little color on side to make more beautiful doesn't bother me
            - כי הצבע הוא מעט מן המעט בגוף הטלית דרובו לבן, mostly white so fulfills דין
- Even though more common ספרדי practice is white stripes, not end of world to have black
    - **Ex:** pictures of רב בנציון אבא שאול with טלית with black stripes
    - **Ex:** pictures of רב כדורי with טלית with black stripes
    - **Ex:** רב עובדיה mostly white but from earlier years some pictures with black stripes also
