# ברכת Craisins – מהו עץ

## הקדמה

- **Case:** what ברכה on cranberries, which people eat as dried sweetened Craisins because raw cranberries are extremely bitter
    - **Q:** boils down to what is a tree, a practical definitional question
- **גמ׳ מסכת ברכות**
    - Tree is where when fruit is removed the גוזא remains intact from year to year and produces fruit in future years
    - If when fruit is removed the גוזא is depleted and gone and will not produce more fruit, it is not a tree
    - **Q:** what is גוזא, what has to stay from year to year to be a tree

## מח׳ רא״ש וגאונים בגדר גוזא

- **רא״ש**
    - גוזא refers to roots
    - If roots continue to exist and bear fruit, it is a tree
    - Vegetable needs replanting every year, does not even have roots remaining from year to year
- **גאונים**
    - גוזא refers to trunk
    - Even if trunk falls off and roots remain intact so no replanting is needed and it grows tall again like a banana tree, still דין of האדמה, still considered vegetable, because trunk does not stay from year to year
- **נ״מ:** banana, where roots remain but trunk does not remain
    - **נ״מ:** not only הלכות ברכות but הלכות ערלה
        - Fruits from first three years of a tree are אסור בהנאה מדין ערלה

## שולחן ערוך ומשנה ברורה

- **שולחן ערוך סימן ר״ג סעיף ב׳**
    - האדמה on strawberries growing on a bush
    - **רמ״א:** because we פסקנן like גאונים that tree bears fruits from trunk, but if fruits regenerate every year only from roots then ברכה is האדמה
        - So ברכה on banana is האדמה
- **משנה ברורה ס״ק ג׳**
    - **Case:** ברכה should have been האדמה because of non-tree characteristics, but looked like tree so said העץ
        - **A:** take small bite so ברכה goes on something, then recite proper ברכה of בורא פרי האדמה
    - In reverse would be יוצא, if ברכה was really supposed to be העץ and said האדמה
- Therefore should be simple for any fruit question whether fruit or vegetable, not by American fruit vs vegetable but by whether גוזא, meaning trunk acc to גאונים, remains from year to year

## ירושלמי וסימנים אחרים

- **ירושלמי מסכת כלאים**
    - Whole גוזא test is only when close call whether tree or vegetable
    - But if obvious by other סימנים that it is a tree, go with what is obvious regardless of trunk regenerating
    - **Q:** ירושלמי never tells what makes it obvious, so without clear גדר how know what is obvious
- **משנה ברורה ס״ק ג׳**
    - Plant with tree characteristics in that it produces fruit every year w/o replanting, even if trunk stays every year, but height is less than three טפחים from ground so looks like vegetable from that perspective, that makes it האדמה
    - That is what ירושלמי refers to, it is obvious, no need to look at גוזא, look how low it is, below three טפחים from ground
- **רב משה בתשובה אגרות משה חלק א׳ סימן פ״ה**
    - That משנה ברורה states מנהג, but that מנהג was not נוהג בכל המקומות
    - If know in place where מנהג to say האדמה on anything growing within three טפחים from ground, can do that, but if do not know in place with such מנהג, should not follow this משנה ברורה criterion
- Other criteria suggested by other ראשונים and mostly אחרונים
    - **שולחן ערוך הרב (אלטר רבי):** depends whether fruit grows directly from trunk, tree defined as having branches, if fruit grows from trunk w/o branches going off trunk that fruit grows from, then vegetable
    - **רדב״ז:** if produces fruit in first year after planting, trees take time till produce fruit while vegetables produce right away, so that סימן vegetable
        - Hence discussions in פוסקים about raspberries and blueberries and eggplants and papayas

## Answering our Questions

- **Case:** cranberries grow on very low bushes producing berries annually, no replanting needed, trunk and roots do not disappear, stays same way, pick berries and new berries grow next year
    - So as far as גוזא concerned it is a tree, but as far as where grows it is within three טפחים of ground
- **רב שלמה זלמן אוירבך**
    - ברכה is האדמה because grows within three טפחים of ground, which לכאורה is what משנה ברורה would say
- **רב אלישיב**
    - Held העץ for two reasons
        - **A1:** maybe רב משה right that we do not פסקנן like that משנה ברורה, only if have מנהג and know מנהג המקום, but if do not know מנהג of Washington Heights or Teaneck or Five Towns, assume העץ
        - **A2:** משנה ברורה says anything below three טפחים is not really considered fruit, but cranberries are sold as fruit and considered fruit, meaning no one would look at it and call it fruit acc to משנה ברורה, but everybody looks at cranberries and calls it fruit, therefore בורא פרי העץ
- **רב בלסקי זכרונו לברכה (acc to OU website)**
    - Should be האדמה because yes has tree criteria in surviving year to year and can go over hundred years like that, but berries grow very near ground so האדמה based on משנה ברורה
    - **נ״מ:** if have cranberry bush in own backyard and see bush higher than three טפחים from ground, then say העץ on that, but mass-produced bags like Craisins all grow very close to ground, therefore האדמה
