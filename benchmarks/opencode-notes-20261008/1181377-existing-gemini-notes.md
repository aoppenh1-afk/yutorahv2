# אכילת בשר ושמחת יום טוב

## הקדמה

- **Q:** is there an obligation to eat meat on יום טוב, or can a person have milchig meals?
- **גמ׳ פסחים דף ק״ט:**
    - בזמן שבית המקדש קיים אין שמחה אלא בבשר, fulfilled through eating the בשר of קרבנות
    - עכשיו שאין בית המקדש קיים אין שמחה אלא ביין
- **תוס׳ ד״ה במה**
    - בזמן המקדש שמחת יום טוב was only with meat, and בזמן הזה it is fulfilled only with wine
        - The גמ׳ meant that בשר of קרבנות was all you needed then, but now we no longer need בשר for שמחת יום טוב and instead fulfill it with יין
    - Everyone is given what brings them joy: women get new clothing, children get nuts or candies, and men get יין

## קושיית הבית יוסף על הרמב״ם

- **רמב״ם (הלכות יום טוב פרק ו׳ הלכה י״ז–י״ח)**
    - All days of יום טוב are forbidden for הספד and תענית, and a person is obligated to be שמח וטוב לב together with his wife, children, and household
    - Even though the biblical שמחה refers to קרבן שלמים, included in that שמחה is to make everyone happy according to what fits them:
        - Children are given קליות, nuts, and sweets
        - Women are bought nice clothes and jewelry according to what he can afford
        - Men eat meat and drink wine, שאין שמחה אלא בבשר ואין שמחה אלא ביין
- **בית יוסף (סימן תקכ״ט)**
    - **Q:** why did the רמב״ם require eating meat along with drinking wine בזמן הזה?
        - The ברייתא in פסחים explicitly says that nowadays אין שמחה אלא ביין, which implies that wine alone suffices without meat

## תירוצי הפוסקים

- **ים של שלמה (ביצה פרק ב׳ סימן ה׳)**
    - **A:** אין שמחה אלא ביין does not exclude eating meat; meat remains the עיקר of שמחה because שמחת יום טוב requires a formal סעודה
        - A סעודה cannot consist of wine alone, and there is no true סעודה or lev tov without eating meat
    - בזמן המקדש, eating בשר שלמים was both physically delicious and a מצוה of a קרבן, which provided a complete measure of שמחה on its own
        - They could drink plain water or heavily diluted wine during the meal and still fulfill their שמחה
    - בזמן הזה, meat has lost the appeal of being a קרבן, so meat alone leaves an incomplete שמחה and reminds a person of the חורבן and גלות
        - The גמ׳ teaches that לא נברא יין אלא לנחם בו את האבלים
        - Since we experience a communal mourning over the חורבן, wine is needed to overcome that sadness and achieve true שמחה
        - Therefore acc to the רמב״ם both meat and wine are required nowadays
- **ב״ח (סימן תקכ״ט)**
    - Proves from other סוגיות that בשר חולין still brings שמחה בזמן הזה:
        - **משנה חולין דף פ״ג. (פרק אותו ואת בנו):** at four times during the year a seller of an animal must inform the buyer if he sold its mother that day, because on ערב יום טוב everyone slaughters meat for the holiday, showing that non-sacrificial meat still provides שמחה
        - **גמ׳ שבת דף קי״ח::** עונג שבת is fulfilled with fatty meat and large fish
            - The distinction between עונג and שמחה is debated (analogous to sefarim named after debated concepts such as עונג יום טוב, קדושת לוי, נחלת שמעון, or שבט הקהתי)
    - **A:** the גמ׳ in פסחים means that בזמן המקדש the עיקר שמחה was בשר שלמים because it had a dual שמחה (the מצוה of the קרבן and the pleasure of meat), whereas wine had only one level of שמחה
        - בזמן הזה, the עיקר שמחה is יין because the פסוק explicitly links wine to joy (״ויין ישמח לבב אנוש״), whereas no explicit פסוק associates בשר חולין with שמחה
        - **תוס׳:** meant that nowadays יין is the primary source of שמחה, not that meat is excluded entirely
- **ערוך לנר (סוכה דף מ״ב.)**
    - **A:** whether meat is required בזמן הזה depends on a מחלוקת תנאים regarding the source for שמחה
        - The גמ׳ in פסחים follows רבי יהודה בן בתירא, who derives שמחה from ״ושמחת בחגך״ (referring to קרבנות), so nowadays without a קרבן only wine applies
        - The רמב״ם follows רב אשי in גמ׳ חגיגה, who holds that even בשר חולין provides שמחה, excluding only birds and מנחות

## שיטת השולחן ערוך והאחרונים

- **שולחן ערוך (סימן תקכ״ט סעיף ב׳)**
    - A person must be happy on the מועד with his family by giving children treats, women clothing and jewelry, and feeding the גר, יתום, and אלמנה
    - Meat is omitted entirely from this סעיף
- **ביאור הלכה**
    - Explains that בזמן הזה there is no strict obligation to eat meat because we lack בשר שלמים, but there is still a מצוה to eat meat since שמחה is associated with it
    - **Q:** why did the מחבר omit meat?
        - **A:** he follows his own view in the בית יוסף that meat is not an absolute requirement בזמן הזה
    - **Q:** why did the מחבר omit wine in סעיף ב׳?
        - **A:** he relies on סעיף א׳, which already requires establishing the meal over wine with קידוש
- **שו״ת יחווה דעת (חלק ו׳ סימן ל״ג)**
    - **בנין שלמה (רבי שלמה הכהן מוילנא)**
        - שמחת יום טוב cannot be defined solely by physical pleasure (הנאת הגוף), because non-sacrificial meat tastes just as good as בשר שלמים
        - Rather, the עיקר שמחה of שלמים was its מצוה and קדושה, which brought joy to both body and soul (שמחה לגוף ולנפש), which ordinary meat lacks
    - **מסקנת הרב עובדיה יוסף**
        - Even בזמן הזה there is a מצוה מן התורה to fulfill שמחה through eating meat and drinking wine acc to the רמב״ם
        - The primary מצוה is with בשר בהמה, but if someone cannot eat beef due to health or כשרות reasons, they can fulfill it with בשר עוף
    - **רמב״ם (הלכות יום טוב פרק ו׳ הלכה י״ח)**
        - When eating and drinking on יום טוב, one must feed the poor, יתומים, and אלמנות
        - If someone locks his doors and feasts only with his own family without caring for the needy, אין זו שמחת מצוה אלא שמחת כריסו, and it is an embarrassment
        - True שמחה requires enabling others to have שמחה, such as providing meals or funding through the local רב prior to יום טוב

## גדרי החיוב למעשה

- **Frequency of eating meat on יום טוב**
    - Rabbi Willig suggested (possibly quoting the שאגת אריה) that meat should be eaten at least once each day of יום טוב
    - **Q:** how are days calculated for this requirement?
        - Usually in הלכה the night precedes the day (היום הולך אחר הלילה)
        - In קדשים the rule is הלילה הולך אחר היום, so a sacrifice is eaten during the day of slaughter and the following night
        - Since שמחת יום טוב stems from בשר שלמים, the schedule may follow the calculation of קדשים:
            - **Case:** a two-day יום טוב starting Tuesday night through Thursday
                - Tuesday night requires meat because it may be the only meal associated with that קדשים cycle
                - Thursday lunch requires meat because it is the only meal of that day's cycle
                - On Wednesday, having meat at either the day meal or Wednesday night suffices, without needing meat at both
- **Guidelines for drinking wine**
    - Wine should not be consumed by children or anyone with alcohol sensitivities
    - Drinking wine is meant to bring שמחה within the framework of a מצוה, such as a סעודת מצוה, יום טוב, קידוש, or שבע ברכות (where Rav Schachter requires either meat or wine)
