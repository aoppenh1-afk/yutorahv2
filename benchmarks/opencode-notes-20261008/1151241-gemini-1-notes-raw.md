# גדר אילן וברכה על חמוציות

## הקדמה

- **Case:** question from a תלמיד about which ברכה to recite on cranberries
    - Cranberries are typically eaten dried and sugared as Craisins because raw cranberries are extremely bitter
    - Practical halachic question boils down to the exact definition of an אילן
- **גמ׳ מסכת ברכות**
    - An אילן is defined as a plant where the גוזא remains intact from year to year after the fruit is removed and continues to bear fruit in future years
    - If the גוזא is depleted and gone when fruit is removed, it is not an אילן
- **Q:** what does גוזא mean, and what must remain from year to year to be considered an אילן?

## מחלוקת הרא״ש והגאונים

- **רא״ש**
    - גוזא refers to the roots
    - If roots survive and continue to bear fruit, it is an אילן; a vegetable must be replanted every year because even its roots do not remain
- **גאונים**
    - גוזא refers to the trunk
    - If the trunk falls off with the fruit and only roots remain, even if roots regenerate annually w/o replanting, it is considered a vegetable and its ברכה is האדמה
- **נ״מ:**
    - **Banana:** roots remain from year to year but trunk dies off, so vegetable acc to גאונים and אילן acc to רא״ש
    - **הלכות ערלה:** fruit of the first 3 years of an אילן is אסור בהנאה מדין ערלה

## פסק השולחן ערוך והמשנה ברורה

- **שולחן ערוך (סימן ר״ג סעיף ב׳)**
    - פסק that bush strawberries are האדמה
    - **רמ״א:** we פסק like the גאונים that an אילן must bear fruit from the trunk; if fruit regenerates annually only from roots, ברכה is האדמה (which is why banana is האדמה)
- **משנה ברורה (ס״ק ג׳)**
    - **Case:** someone mistakenly said העץ on an item that is האדמה due to non-tree characteristics
        - **דין:** take a small bite so the ברכה takes effect on something, then recite בורא פרי האדמה
        - If someone mistakenly recited האדמה on an העץ fruit, he is יוצא בדיעבד
    - Halachic status depends on whether the trunk (גוזא acc to גאונים) remains, not American culinary definitions

## ירושלמי כלאים וסימנים נוספים

- **ירושלמי מסכת כלאים**
    - Criterion of גוזא applies only in a close call; if other obvious סימנים show it is an אילן, follow what is obvious regardless of trunk regeneration
    - **Q:** what makes something obviously an אילן, given that the ירושלמי gives no clear גדר?
- **משנה ברורה (ס״ק ג׳)**
    - If a plant keeps producing fruit annually w/o replanting and trunk remains, but it grows less than 3 טפחים from the ground, it looks like a vegetable and is האדמה (this is what the ירושלמי meant by obvious)
- **אגרות משה (חלק א׳ סימן פ״ה)**
    - The 3 טפחים rule was a specific מנהג that was not נוהג בכל המקומות
    - Only follow it if you know your location has that מנהג; otherwise do not follow this criteria of the משנה ברורה
- Other criteria in אחרונים
    - **שולחן ערוך הרב (אלטר רבי):** an אילן must have branches; if fruit grows directly from the trunk w/o branches, it is a vegetable
    - **רדב״ז:** if a plant produces fruit in its first year, it is a vegetable, because trees take time to bear fruit while vegetables produce immediately in year one (applied by פוסקים to blueberries, raspberries, eggplants, and papayas)

## ברכה על Craisins

- **Status of cranberries:** low bushes where trunk and roots remain from year to year, so by גוזא it is an אילן, but it grows within 3 טפחים of the ground
- **רב שלמה זלמן אוירבך**
    - פסק that the ברכה is האדמה because it grows within 3 טפחים of the ground, following the משנה ברורה
- **רב אלישיב**
    - Held the ברכה is העץ for 2 reasons:
        - Acc to אגרות משה we do not follow the משנה ברורה without an established מנהג המקום, and in communities like Washington Heights, Teaneck, or the Five Towns where מנהג is unknown, assume העץ
        - משנה ברורה says below 3 טפחים is not considered a fruit because people would not call it one, but cranberries are commercially sold and universally recognized as a fruit
- **רב בלסקי**
    - Mass-produced cranberries (Craisins) grow very low to the ground, so ברכה is האדמה acc to משנה ברורה even though the plant survives over 100 years
    - If someone grows a cranberry bush in a backyard higher than 3 טפחים from the ground, ברכה is העץ
- **Rav Gedaliah Dov Schwartz**
    - Also ruled האדמה, noting it is less risky because האדמה covers העץ בדיעבד
