# ברכה on Cranberries – מהו אילן

## הקדמה

- **Case:** what ברכה on cranberries, which people eat dried and sugared as Craisins because raw cranberries are extremely bitter
    - **Q:** this boils down to what is a tree
- **גמ׳ מסכת ברכות**
    - Tree is defined by גוזא remaining intact from year to year and continuing to produce fruit in future years
        - If when fruit is removed the גוזא is depleted and gone and will not produce more fruit, then not a tree
    - **Q:** what is גוזא, what has to stay from year to year to be a tree

## שיטת הרא״ש

- **רא״ש**
    - גוזא refers to roots
        - If roots continue to exist and bear fruit, considered a tree
        - Vegetable is something that needs replanting every year, does not even have roots remaining from year to year

## שיטת הגאונים

- **גאונים**
    - גוזא refers to trunk
        - Even if roots remain intact and no replanting needed because roots regrow it very tall like a banana tree, still not a tree but האדמה, considered a vegetable, because trunk does not stay from year to year
- **נ״מ:** banana, where roots remain but trunk does not remain
- **נ״מ:** not only הלכות ברכות but הלכות ערלה
    - Fruits from first three years of a tree are אסור בהנאה מדין ערלה
- **שולחן ערוך סימן ר״ג סעיף ב׳**
    - Say האדמה on strawberries that grow on a bush
        - **רמ״א:** because we פסקן like הגאונים, definition of tree is something bearing fruits from trunk, but if fruits regenerate every year only from roots then ברכה is האדמה
        - So ברכה on banana is האדמה
- **משנה ברורה ס״ק ג׳**
    - If ברכה should have been האדמה because it has characteristics of non-tree but you thought it looked like a tree so said העץ, take little bite so ברכה goes on something, then recite proper ברכה, then go back and recite בורא פרי האדמה
        - In reverse would not be case, if ברכה really supposed to be העץ and said האדמה would be יוצא

## ירושלמי מסכת כלאים – obvious tree vs vegetable

- **ירושלמי מסכת כלאים**
    - Whole thing about גוזא is only when close call whether tree or vegetable
        - But if obvious, if other סימנים make it obvious that it is a tree, then regardless of trunk regenerating or not go with obvious
    - **Q:** ירושלמי never tells what makes it obvious, so without clear גדר how to know what is obvious
- **משנה ברורה ס״ק ג׳**
    - Plant with characteristics of tree in that it continues to produce fruit every year without replanting, and even if trunk stays in place every year, but height so low to ground, less than three טפחים from ground, that it looks like vegetable from that perspective, makes it האדמה
        - That is what ירושלמי refers to, come on it is obvious, do not need to look whether גוזא reproduces from year to year, look how low it is, below three טפחים from ground, that makes it האדמה
- **רב משה in תשובה in אגרות משה חלק א׳ סימן פ״ה**
    - That is the מנהג that משנה ברורה says, but that מנהג was not נוהג בכל המקומות
        - If know you are in place where they had such מנהג to say האדמה on anything growing within three טפחים from ground, can do that
        - But if do not know you are in place that had such מנהג, should not follow this criteria of משנה ברורה
- **שולחן ערוך הרב (אלטר רבי)**
    - Depends whether fruit grows directly from trunk, tree defined as having branches
        - If fruit grows from trunk and does not have branches going off trunk that fruit grows from, then not tree, then vegetable
- **רדב״ז**
    - If produces fruit in first year after planting, trees do not typically do that, trees take time till produce fruit, vegetables produce fruit right away in first year, so that סימן that it is vegetable if produces fruit right away in first year
        - This is why discussions in פוסקים about raspberries and blueberries and eggplants and papayas and all sorts of interesting fruits

## Cranberries

- **Case:** cranberries grow typically on very low bushes that produce berries annually, so no need to replant, and trunk does not disappear, roots do not disappear, stays exactly way it was, pick off berries and new berries grow next year
    - So as far as גוזא concerned it is a tree, but as far as where it grows it is within three טפחים of ground
- **רב שלמה זלמן אוירבך**
    - ברכה is האדמה because grows within three טפחים of ground, and that לכאורה would be what משנה ברורה would say as well
- **רב אלישיב**
    - Held העץ for two reasons
        - **A1:** maybe רב משה right that we do not פסקן like that משנה ברורה, and only if have מנהג and in place where know that is מנהג המקום, but if do not know מנהג of Washington Heights or מנהג of Teaneck or Five Towns, then just assume it is העץ
        - **A2:** משנה ברורה says anything growing below three טפחים is not really considered fruit, but cranberries are often sold as fruit and considered fruit, meaning משנה ברורה seems to say no one would look at it and call it fruit, but everybody looks at cranberries and calls it fruit, therefore ברכה should be בורא פרי העץ
- **רב בלסקי זכרונו לברכה (acc to OU website)**
    - Should be האדמה because yes has criteria of tree in that survives from year to year, and could go for over hundred years like that, but berries grow very near ground and it is האדמה based on משנה ברורה
        - However if have cranberry bush in own backyard and see that cranberry bush higher than three טפחים from ground, then say העץ on that
        - But ones mass-produced that get in bags, Craisins, those all grow very close to ground, therefore ברכה should be האדמה
- **Rav Gedaliah Dov Schwartz (שמעתי)**
    - ברכה should be האדמה, little less risky to say האדמה than to say העץ
