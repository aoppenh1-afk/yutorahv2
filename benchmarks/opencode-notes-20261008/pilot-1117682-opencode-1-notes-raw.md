# Stripes on the טלית

## הקדמה

- **Case:** many people have black stripes on the טלית, some have blue, some have white, and some have all different colors
    - **Q:** where does this come from, is it a מנהג ישראל or just something we do
- **Source:** three possibilities mentioned by the ספרים
    - **פרי מגדים סימן ט׳:**
        - Stripes are a זכר לתכלת, because for many years the Jewish people did not have תכלת
            - **Q:** now that I have תכלת, should I not have stripes on my טלית
            - **A:** you are still allowed to have stripes on your טלית
    - **נ״מ:** only a wool טלית קטן will have black stripes, but a cotton טלית קטן does not have black stripes
        - Not because they cannot make black cotton, since they make black cotton t-shirts
        - Because תכלת only works on wool, since תכלת is by definition wool, so black stripes as a זכר לתכלת have to be on a wool בגד
    - **זוהר:**
        - Based on קבלה, black represents the מדה of גבורה and white represents the מדה of חסד, or רחמים
            - So there is this fusion of גבורה and רחמים represented in the טלית, therefore it is not just white, which is the primary color, but also black or blue as the secondary color
    - **משנה מסכת ברכות:**
        - משנה talks about the different זמנים in the morning, and the earliest זמן to say קריאת שמע is when a person is able to be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
            - If you have it right in front of you and are able to see that you have a white בגד and a תכלת stripe on it, then you can see if you are able to be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
            - So maybe that is why it was included on the טלית, not only as a זכר to the תכלת, but to function for one of the things תכלת helped with, to tell when the זמן is for קריאת שמע
                - It cannot function as the מצוה דאורייתא of תכלת

## Black stripes

- **Summary:** three possible sources, either זכר לתכלת, or על פי קבלה, or to be מבחין בין תכלת ללבן
    - **Q:** what is the basis for each of the different colors, and the most common one nowadays is black
- **רמב״ם הלכות ציצית פרק ב׳:**
    - רמב״ם held that the דם החלזון they would use was a black דם, although רמב״ם seems to understand that once it went through the process it actually produced a blue dye and not a black dye
- **גמ׳ מנחות:**
    - תכלת is דומה לים and ים is דומה לרקיע, so תכלת in some way is supposed to be the color of the ים or the color of the רקיע
        - But גמ׳ does not say explicitly at what time of day
            - רקיע has a very different color during broad daylight than at nighttime
                - **רש״י:** it is the רקיע toward evening time, which would mean a much darker blue
                - **רמב״ם:** it is the middle of the day
    - **נ״מ:** if you hold תכלת is supposed to be black, then the stripes are supposed to be black, or even if תכלת were a blackish color, a very dark blue that almost appears black
- **תורה is אש שחורה על גבי אש לבנה:**
    - Sense of the לבנה, the white, which is purity and innocence, and the black lines, the letters of the תורה, are the אש שחורה on top of the אש לבנה, and stripes are representative of that
        - Maybe somehow representative of תורה, because the ציצית themselves are supposed to represent all תרי״ג מצות
            - **רש״י בחומש:** ציצית represent all תרי״ג מצות
            - **גמ׳:** it is supposed to remind us of all תרי״ג מצות
            - So maybe the בגד upon which you put the ציצית should somehow represent the entirety of תורה

## Blue stripes

- Blue used to be the more common מנהג, but it grew out of favor in more contemporary times
    - **Ex:** David Wolffsohn, a prominent Zionist businessman, when Theodor Herzl's proposed design for the Israeli flag was not gaining support, wrote that at the behest of Herzl he came to Basel to prepare for the Zionist Congress and asked what flag to hang in the Congress Hall
        - Then an idea struck him: we have a flag, and it is blue and white, the טלית with which we wrap ourselves when we pray, that is our symbol, so take the טלית out of the bag, slap a מגן דוד on it, and that is our flag
        - That was the concept of the Israeli flag, and the assumption was that the טלית had blue stripes on it

## White stripes

- **Case:** on יום כיפור people wear white, as a symbol of purity, that אם יהיו חטאיכם כשנים כשלג ילבינו, that even though we may have sins, we hope it becomes whitened and purified
    - But even not on יום כיפור, there seems to be a fairly common ספרדי practice to have white stripes on the טלית
- **ברכי יוסף בשיורי ברכה בשם מהרש״ל:**
    - **יש אומרים** that the טלית should be white
- **שולחן ערוך סימן ט׳ סעיף ה׳:**
    - יש אומרים שצריך לעשות הציצית מצבע הטלית, והמדקדקים נוהגים כן
        - **רמ״א:** והאשכנזים לא נהגו לעשות הציצית רק לבנים, אף בבגדים צבועים, ואין לשנות
        - Put two things together: number one, the ציצית are supposed to match the בגד, and number two, רמ״א writes our מנהג is we only use white ציצית, so if the ציצית match the בגד and you only use white ציצית, then the בגד should be white
- **על פי קבלה:** many ספרדים have דווקא fully white
    - **בן איש חי פרשת נח:**
        - יזהר לעשות הטליות והחוטים של הציציות הכל לבן, בין בטלית גדול בין בטלית קטן, רמז לדבר עטוף בלבן
        - וטליתות שיש בהן צבע מצדדים לנוי, לא אכפת, if there is a little color on the side to make it more beautiful, that does not bother me
            - כי הצבע הוא מעט מן המעט בגוף הטלית דרובו לבן, it is mostly white, so that fulfills the דין
- Even though the more common ספרדי practice is to have white stripes, it is not the end of the world to have black
    - **Ex:** there are pictures of רב בנציון אבא שאול with a טלית with black stripes on it
    - **Ex:** there are pictures of רב כדורי with a טלית with black stripes on it
    - **Ex:** רב עובדיה is mostly white, but from the earlier years there are pictures of him with a טלית with black stripes also
