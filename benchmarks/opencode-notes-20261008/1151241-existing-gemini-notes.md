# הגדרת אילן לענין ברכות

## הקדמה

- **Case:** a תלמיד asked what ברכה to make on cranberries (specifically Craisins)
    - Cranberries are extremely bitter raw, so people typically eat them dried and sugared
- **Q:** what is the definition of an אילן?
- **גמ׳ ברכות**
    - An אילן is defined as a plant where when you remove the fruit, the גוזא remains intact from year to year and continues to produce fruit
    - If the גוזא is depleted and gone when the fruit is removed, it is not an אילן
    - **Q:** what is the definition of גוזא?

## הגדרת גוזא

- **רא״ש**
    - גוזא refers to the roots
    - If the roots remain from year to year and bear fruit, it is considered an אילן
    - A vegetable has to be replanted every year because even its roots do not remain
- **גאונים**
    - גוזא refers to the trunk
    - If the trunk falls off and only the roots remain, it is considered a vegetable (ברכתו האדמה) even if it regrows tall every year w/o replanting
- **נ״מ:**
    - Banana: the roots remain annually but the trunk does not, so acc to the רא״ש it would be העץ and acc to the גאונים it is האדמה
    - הלכות ערלה: fruit of a tree is אסור בהנאה for the first three years מדין ערלה, which depends on whether it has the דין of an אילן
- **שולחן ערוך (סימן ר״ג סעיף ב׳) ורמ״א**
    - שולחן ערוך פסקנ's האדמה on strawberries on a bush
    - רמ״א explains we פסקן like the גאונים that an אילן must bear fruit from a trunk that remains from year to year
    - Therefore the ברכה on a banana is האדמה
- **משנה ברורה (ס״ק ג׳)**
    - **Case:** said העץ on something that is really האדמה because it looked like a tree
        - Take a little bite so the ברכה is not לבטלה, and then recite בורא פרי האדמה
    - If one recited האדמה on העץ בדיעבד, one is יוצא

## ירושלמי כלאים and Other Criteria

- **ירושלמי כלאים**
    - The criteria of גוזא only applies when it is a close call
    - If there are obvious סימנים that it is an אילן or a vegetable, follow what is obvious regardless of whether the trunk remains
    - **Q:** the ירושלמי never defines what makes it obvious or what the clear גדר is
- **משנה ברורה (ס״ק ג׳)**
    - Explains that the ירושלמי refers to height: if a plant is less than three טפחים from the ground, it looks like a vegetable and its ברכה is האדמה
        - Even if it produces fruit annually w/o replanting and its trunk remains intact
- **אגרות משה (חלק א׳ סימן פ״ה)**
    - The rule cited by the משנה ברורה was a specific מנהג that was not נוהג בכל המקומות
    - Follow it only if you know you are in a place that has that מנהג; otherwise do not use the height criteria of the משנה ברורה
- Other criteria among אחרונים:
    - **אלטר רבי (שולחן ערוך הרב):** an אילן must have branches; if fruit grows directly off the trunk w/o branches, it is a vegetable
    - **רדב״ז:** if it produces fruit in its first year, it is a vegetable, because trees take time to produce fruit
    - These criteria form the basis for discussions on raspberries, blueberries, eggplants, and papayas

## ברכה on Cranberries

- **Case:** cranberries (and Craisins)
    - Grow on very low bushes producing berries annually w/o replanting, and both trunk and roots remain intact
    - Acc to the criteria of גוזא it is an אילן, but it grows within three טפחים of the ground
- **רב שלמה זלמן אוירבך**
    - פסקנ'd האדמה because it grows within three טפחים of the ground, following the משנה ברורה
- **רב אלישיב**
    - Held the ברכה is העץ based on two סברות:
        - Acc to רב משה we only follow the משנה ברורה if there is a known מנהג המקום, and in places like Washington Heights, Teaneck, or the Five Towns where the מנהג is unknown, assume העץ
        - The משנה ברורה classified below three טפחים as האדמה because people do not view it as fruit, but cranberries are universally sold and recognized as fruit
- **רב בלסקי (OU)**
    - פסקנ'd האדמה on mass-produced Craisins because the berries grow within three טפחים of the ground, following the משנה ברורה, even though the bush survives annually (up to 100+ years)
    - If someone grows a cranberry bush in a backyard that reaches higher than three טפחים, the ברכה would be העץ
- **Rav Gedaliah Dov Schwartz**
    - Also reported to hold האדמה, noting it is less halachically risky than saying העץ
