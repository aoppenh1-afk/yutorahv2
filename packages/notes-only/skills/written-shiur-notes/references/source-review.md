# Human source review

Review the complete notes against the frozen full transcript. Use the transcript as the reference. Record the reviewer, source hash, note hash, profile, decision, and exact evidence for every defect. Keep this audit separate from the notes artifact.

Check the beginning, middle, and final substantive passage. Account for each developed argument, distinct proof, objection, competing view, qualification, application, and unresolved inquiry in lecture order. Check attribution, quotations, citation precision, case relationships, and the speaker's degree of certainty. A familiar source does not justify filling an unclear identifier or reconstructing a quotation.

Check connected English prose with identifiable Hebrew terminology in unvocalized Hebrew script, the teaching voice, source-derived headings, and the supplied output contract. Concision is useful only while substantive coverage and useful teaching voice remain intact.

For each finding, quote the relevant source and note passages, identify the change in meaning or contract violation, and mark its severity. Invented or altered substantive content is critical. Distinguish transcription uncertainty from a claim made by the speaker. An unresolved source uncertainty must remain precise and local.

Return accepted or rejected with reasons. Structural validity and an AI review are separate observations; neither records human acceptance. For matched route review, hide route labels from the human reviewer where practical.
